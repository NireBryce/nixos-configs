# 13. Reversibility can stand in for a decision

_Last modified: 2026-09-29_

§13 of [lessons-learned.md](../lessons-learned.md), the index that files every lesson by when it applies; this is §13's full account.

Three times the user declined a fork and asked for a documented escape hatch:

or documenting.**
